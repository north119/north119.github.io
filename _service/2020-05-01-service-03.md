### Project: "Jing Shi Zhu Xin" - Mental Health Support and Innovation System

*   **Assessment Framework Development**: 
    *   Designed the comprehensive framework for the psychological assessment system.
    *   Collected and synthesized professional psychological scales to generate detailed feedback reports for users.
*   **Scale Development & Refinement**:
    *   Assisted in developing a **comprehensive family functioning scale** and a mental health screening checklist for adolescents.
    *   Conducted extensive literature reviews and item generation based on established psychometric theories.
    *   Coordinated data collection from primary and secondary school students nationwide via a dedicated app platform.
    *   Performed **scale refinement and validation** based on the collected empirical data.
*   **Honors**: Awarded the **Second Prize** in the 7th China International "Internet+" College Students' Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition (Beijing Division).

**Research Skills & Social Impact:** 
This project bridge the gap between psychometric theory and practical application. By leveraging mobile technology, we facilitated the mental health screening process for thousands of students, demonstrating my ability to manage large-scale data collection and contribute to scale validation.
