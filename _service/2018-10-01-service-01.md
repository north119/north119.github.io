### Key Responsibilities and Achievements

*   **Public Account Management**: Operated and managed the official WeChat public account for the Mental Health Association, primarily overseeing the science communication section.
*   **Science Communication & Editing**: 
    *   Independently authored 7 popular science articles on topics such as **vicarious trauma** and claustrophobia.
    *   Reviewed and edited over 30 articles and managed the layout/typesetting for more than 15 posts.
    *   Contributed professional content to the *Student Health Newspaper*.
*   **Event Organization**: Successfully organized specialized science popularization lectures on **Music Therapy** and academic salons focused on **Intimate Relationships**.
